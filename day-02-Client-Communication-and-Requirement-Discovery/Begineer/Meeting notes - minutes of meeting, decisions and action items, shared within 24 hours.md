
# Meeting Notes: Minutes of Meeting, Decisions, and Action Items

## 1. What Are Meeting Notes?

Meeting notes are written records of the important discussions, decisions, questions, and tasks covered during a meeting.

They help everyone understand what was discussed, what was agreed upon, and what needs to happen next.

**Why are meeting notes important?**
- Keep everyone informed.
- Prevent misunderstandings.
- Record important decisions.
- Clarify responsibilities and deadlines.
- Track pending tasks.
- Provide a reference for future discussions.

## 2. Minutes of Meeting (MoM)

Minutes of Meeting (MoM) are structured records of a meeting. They summarize the main discussions, decisions, and actions that need to be taken.

They are usually prepared by the project manager, business analyst, or an assigned team member.

**A Minutes of Meeting document should include:**

1. **Meeting Details:** Meeting title, date, time, participants, and location or platform.
2. **Agenda:** The main topics planned for discussion.
3. **Discussion Summary:** A brief summary of the important points discussed.
4. **Decisions Made:** The agreements and conclusions reached during the meeting.
5. **Action Items:** Tasks that need to be completed, along with their owners and deadlines.
6. **Pending Questions:** Issues that still need clarification.
7. **Next Meeting:** The date and time of the next meeting, if scheduled.

## 3. Decisions vs. Action Items

These are two important parts of meeting documentation, but they serve different purposes.

**Decisions** are agreements or conclusions reached during a meeting. They record what the client or team has approved or agreed upon.

**Action Items** are tasks that need to be completed after the meeting. Each task should have a clear owner, deadline, and status.

The main difference is:

- Decisions explain **what has been agreed upon**.
- Action items explain **what needs to be done next**.

## 4. Why Should Meeting Notes Be Shared Within 24 Hours?

Meeting notes should ideally be shared within 24 hours while the discussion is still fresh.

This helps:
- Prevent misunderstandings by allowing participants to review and correct the notes.
- Maintain accountability by making responsibilities clear.
- Keep the project moving by allowing team members to start their tasks.
- Preserve important decisions and agreements.
- Keep everyone informed, including those who missed the meeting.

## 5. How to Prepare and Share Meeting Notes

A project manager should follow these steps after an important meeting:

1. **Take Notes:** Record important discussions, decisions, questions, and tasks during the meeting.
2. **Organize Information:** Separate the notes into discussions, decisions, action items, and pending questions.
3. **Confirm Details:** Check that decisions, responsibilities, and deadlines are correct. Clarify anything uncertain.
4. **Prepare MoM:** Turn the rough notes into a clear, structured document.
5. **Share Within 24 Hours:** Send the notes to relevant participants through email or a shared project platform.
6. **Follow Up:** Track action items and check whether assigned tasks are completed on time.

## 6. Where Should Meeting Notes Be Stored?

Meeting notes should be stored in a shared location where authorized project members can easily access them.

Common tools include:

- **Google Docs:** Writing and sharing meeting notes.
- **Notion:** Organizing project documentation.
- **Confluence:** Maintaining formal project records.
- **Microsoft Teams:** Sharing meeting summaries.
- **Jira:** Connecting action items to project tasks.

A good practice is to organize meeting notes by project and date so previous discussions and decisions are easy to find.

## 7. Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Sharing notes too late:** Important details may be forgotten.
- **Writing everything word for word:** Notes become unnecessarily long.
- **Missing decisions:** Team members may disagree about what was approved.
- **Not assigning task owners:** Nobody knows who is responsible.
- **Leaving out deadlines:** Tasks may be delayed.
- **Ignoring pending questions:** Important requirements may remain unclear.
- **Poor documentation storage:** Team members may struggle to find information.
- **Not following up:** Assigned tasks may never get completed.

## 8. Key Takeaways

Meeting notes help keep project communication clear and organized.

A good Minutes of Meeting document should record the important discussions, decisions, action items, responsible people, and deadlines.

Remember:

- **MoM:** Records what happened during the meeting.
- **Decisions:** Record what was agreed upon.
- **Action Items:** Record what needs to be done, who will do it, and when.

Meeting notes should ideally be shared within 24 hours and stored in a shared project location.

**Remember:** A productive meeting should lead to clear decisions, assigned responsibilities, and follow-up actions.
