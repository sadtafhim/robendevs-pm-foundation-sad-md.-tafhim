
# Team Roles and Responsibilities

When developing a software product, different teams work together, and each team has its own responsibilities and areas of ownership. Understanding these roles helps ensure smooth collaboration and successful product delivery.

## Team Responsibilities

| Team | Primary Responsibility | What They Own | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Client** | Define business needs and expectations. | Business goals, requirements, domain knowledge, feedback, scope decisions, and final approval. | Explains the business requirements for an e-commerce application, including product sales, payments, and order tracking. |
| **Designer (UI/UX)** | Design the product's appearance and user experience. | User research, user flows, wireframes, UI designs, prototypes, design systems, and usability. | Designs the home page, product listing, shopping cart, and checkout screens. |
| **Engineers (Development Team)** | Build the actual software. | Frontend development, backend development, database implementation, APIs, third-party integrations, code quality, and technical implementation. | Develops product browsing, shopping cart, payment processing, and order management functionality. |
| **QA (Quality Assurance)** | Ensure software quality and verify that requirements are met. | Test planning, functional testing, integration testing, regression testing, performance testing, bug reporting, and defect verification. | Tests whether users can register, log in, purchase products, and receive order confirmations correctly. |
| **DevOps (Development and Operations)** | Manage infrastructure, deployment, and operational reliability. | CI/CD pipelines, cloud infrastructure, deployment, monitoring, security configurations, scalability, backups, and recovery. | Configures cloud infrastructure, automates application deployment, and monitors production performance. |
